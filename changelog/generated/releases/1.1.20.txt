TypeSync 1.1.20 - Home Widgets + Editor Readability
Important:
- TypeSync adds configurable note widgets on the Home page and new Android home-screen widgets for recently opened, frequently opened, and largest notes.
New Features:
- Added a swipeable Home Page Widgets carousel, with settings to choose the Upcoming, Recently opened, Frequently opened, and Largest notes views.
- Added local note-open history, powering recently and frequently opened note lists without changing synced note data.
- Added Android home-screen widgets for recently opened, frequently opened, and largest notes; the largest-notes widget can compare size, character count, or line count.
Fixes & Improvements:
- Refined note and folder background colors for calmer, more readable surfaces while preserving compatibility with saved legacy colors.
- Improved contrast for note text and icons across selectable background colors.
- Restored strikethrough formatting in the editor toolbar and corrected note-background selection state.
- Polished widget layout and carousel animation for a more compact, stable Home experience.
Notes:
- These notes summarize user-facing changes introduced after `v1.1.19` (2026-08-05).
